Weather Tips

Small ideas to take care of yourself in today's weather.

☀️ Sunny days: Drink plenty of water, wear something light, and enjoy fresh air.

☁️ Cloudy days: Take a gentle walk and let yourself breathe without rushing.

🌧️ Rainy days: Make a warm drink, listen to the rain, and allow yourself to slow down.

❄️ Cold days: Wear cozy layers, eat something warm, and be gentle with yourself.

🌬️ Windy days: Lean on something stable—like music, routine, or comfort food.
